What is this app?

😤

Something feels unfair

Kids can be the plaintiff and file a complaint. A civil trial that turns hurt feelings into words.

🚨

A house rule was broken

When a family rule is broken, a prosecutor brings the case. The judge rules guilty, not guilty or leniency.

🧑‍⚖️

Fair roles for everyone

Only someone who is not the plaintiff or defendant can be the judge. Everyone has the right to choose counsel.

📖

Our family law book

Write your own family rules together, then follow each case from trial to verdict to promise.

How it works

  1. Decide your family laws together and write them in the law book.
  2. File a civil case for something unfair, or a criminal case when a rule is broken.
  3. Choose the roles of plaintiff, defendant, judge and counsel, and open court.
  4. Hear each side, then the judge hands down a verdict.
  5. Set a promise based on the verdict and tick it off once it is kept.
🔒

Safe for kids

No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your family, law book and trial records are stored only on your device and never sent anywhere. The developer only receives feedback you choose to send from Settings and anonymous usage statistics that cannot identify anyone. See the Privacy Policy for details.
